# Luxury 2-Storey House — Cinematic Walkthrough Production Brief

## Creative target
Create a premium 4K single-take-feeling walkthrough inspired by product-storytelling sites: architectural precision, slow camera movement, controlled light, richly furnished rooms, and enough negative space for editorial copy overlays.

The master should be rendered at 3840x2160, 24 or 30 fps, with separate web derivatives. The web experience uses the rendered footage as a numbered Canvas image sequence so scrolling becomes reversible playback.

## House concept
Modern two-storey private residence with warm minimalism:
- Exterior: limestone, charcoal metal, warm oak, floor-to-ceiling low-iron glass.
- Interior palette: warm white plaster, travertine/limestone, smoked oak, muted taupe, charcoal, brushed bronze.
- Landscape: architectural grasses, olive/citrus trees, sculptural shrubs, dark stone, water feature and reflecting pool.
- Lighting arc: blue-hour exterior -> warm dusk -> controlled interior evening glow -> soft night terrace reveal.

## Shot-by-shot direction

### 01 — Arrival / Exterior
1. Begin 20–30 m from the home at blue hour.
2. Slow stabilized dolly toward the front gate.
3. Reveal the full facade only after the first architectural beat.
4. Pass the gate line and track toward the main entrance.
5. Include landscape foreground for depth: grasses, low shrubs and stone.
6. Let interior light spill through glazing without clipping highlights.
7. Finish with the front door centered slightly off-axis so typography can occupy the opposite side.

### 02 — Foyer
Furniture and dressing:
- Sculptural console table in travertine or dark oak.
- Oversized circular/organic mirror.
- Low ceramic vessel with branch arrangement.
- One statement bench in taupe boucle or saddle leather.
- Large-format limestone/travertine flooring.
- Floating staircase with oak treads and glass/metal balustrade.
- Large abstract artwork on the double-height wall.
- Minimal floor sculpture.

Camera:
- Cross the threshold slowly.
- Tilt/pan upward to expose the double-height volume.
- Arc around the staircase.
- Brief detail pass over stone, timber and metal junctions.

Lighting:
- Recessed architectural lighting.
- Warm 2700–3000K practicals.
- Soft concealed stair tread lighting.

### 03 — Living room
Furniture and dressing:
- Large modular low-profile sofa in warm greige.
- Two sculptural lounge chairs.
- Travertine/stone coffee table.
- Textured wool rug.
- Low side tables in dark wood or bronze.
- Full-height curtains with subtle weave.
- Large abstract art grouping.
- Linear fireplace or monolithic stone fireplace wall.
- Minimal books, ceramic objects and a single branch arrangement.
- Floor-to-ceiling glazing with garden/pool view.

Camera:
- Enter from foyer and widen into the room.
- Track past the sofa to reveal the fireplace.
- Slow orbit around the coffee table.
- End toward the glazing to establish indoor/outdoor connection.

### 04 — Kitchen
Furniture and dressing:
- 3–4 m waterfall island in veined stone.
- Eight counter stools or four oversized stools depending on scale.
- Fluted oak or smoked-oak cabinetry.
- Integrated refrigerator, oven and dishwasher panels.
- Brass/bronze hardware kept minimal.
- Two or three sculptural pendant lights.
- Built-in wine storage or display niche.
- Ceramic fruit bowl, espresso setup and restrained countertop objects.
- Secondary prep kitchen opening kept visually clean.

Camera:
- Slide from living room toward island.
- Macro pass across stone veining.
- Low-height pass along cabinet textures.
- Rise gently to reveal dining room beyond.

### 05 — Dining + patio
Dining room:
- Solid timber/stone dining table for 8–10.
- Upholstered dining chairs in taupe/stone.
- Oversized linear pendant or sculptural chandelier.
- Sideboard with art/object styling.
- One large-scale art piece.

Patio:
- Outdoor lounge sofa.
- Two lounge chairs.
- Stone coffee table.
- Outdoor dining extension.
- Built-in grill or outdoor kitchen, kept secondary.
- Slim fire feature.
- Architectural planters.
- Reflecting pool / infinity edge.

Camera:
- Track around dining table.
- Cross threshold through open sliding glass.
- Continue uninterrupted onto covered terrace.
- Reveal pool and garden.
- Finish on a long horizontal composition with house lights reflected in water.

### 06 — Primary suite
Bedroom furniture:
- Upholstered king bed with oversized headboard wall.
- Two compact bedside tables.
- Designer bedside lamps or concealed wall lighting.
- Low bench at bed foot.
- Lounge chair and side table.
- Large area rug.
- Built-in wardrobe wall with timber/slatted details.
- Minimal console and one art piece.

Balcony:
- Two lounge chairs.
- Small stone side table.
- Landscape or skyline view.

Camera:
- Travel upstairs from staircase landing.
- Enter bedroom on a diagonal, not straight-on.
- Pass close to textiles and timber.
- Open toward balcony/window.
- Use a gentle depth shift to make the room feel expansive.

### 07 — Primary bath + terrace
Bathroom:
- Bookmatched stone feature wall.
- Floating double vanity in stone/wood.
- Backlit mirror.
- Wall-mounted brushed bronze fixtures.
- Freestanding sculptural tub.
- Large-format stone floor.
- Frameless shower glass.
- Folded neutral towels and restrained bath accessories.
- Single branch arrangement or stone vessel.

Terrace:
- Outdoor daybed.
- Two sculptural chairs.
- Low table.
- Architectural planter.
- Small water/fire feature depending on concept.

Camera:
- Macro detail over stone and fixtures.
- Pull wide to reveal entire bath.
- Transition through terrace doors.
- Slow rise or crane-like push to open the skyline.

### 08 — Final hero / private tour
- Return to a broad exterior or elevated terrace composition.
- House fully illuminated, pool reflections active, landscape lights subtle.
- Hold the hero framing long enough for CTA copy.
- Keep the preferred CTA-safe negative space on the left or right third.
- Final 2–3 seconds should be visually stable for a premium ending state.

## Furniture / styling rule
Every visible room must feel inhabited but not cluttered. Use a maximum of 1–2 accent objects per major furniture group. Avoid repeated props, duplicate art, floating objects, dead corners or obviously empty staging.

## Camera and lens language
- Exterior hero: 24–28 mm equivalent.
- Interior establishing shots: 20–24 mm equivalent.
- Medium furniture passes: 28–35 mm equivalent.
- Material/detail inserts: 50–85 mm equivalent.
- Keep verticals controlled; use architectural tilt/shift discipline.
- Avoid aggressive handheld motion; the luxury language is composed and deliberate.

## Lighting direction
Use physically believable lighting. Do not rely on a single HDRI alone.
- Large soft key sources through windows.
- Practical lights motivate interior warmth.
- Subtle hidden fill to retain shadow detail.
- Exterior facade grazers for stone texture.
- Pool and water should produce restrained moving reflections.
- Keep exposure transitions smooth between rooms.

## Rendering / post
- 3840x2160 master.
- 24/30 fps.
- Motion blur only where physically motivated.
- Mild filmic contrast and grain.
- Preserve highlight rolloff and natural reflections.
- Render clean plates plus grade references so web compression can be tuned separately.

## Web derivatives
Master footage is not pushed directly to the browser as the entire 4K source. Generate:
- Desktop film sequence: 2560px long edge, WebP/AVIF where validated.
- High quality desktop sequence: optional 3840px source when bandwidth allows.
- Mobile: portrait/alternate crop video clips per chapter.
- Poster: 1600–2400px WebP.
- Optional low-power still fallback.

## Frame naming
`public/house/frames/frame_0001.webp` through `frame_0360.webp`

## Mobile clips
`public/house/mobile/01-arrival.mp4` through `08-private-tour.mp4`

Each mobile clip should be short, muted, loopable and composed as a self-contained room moment. Use IntersectionObserver to play only when visible.

## Audio concept
For the final production pass, create:
- low-level architectural room tone;
- subtle door / footstep / HVAC ambience;
- restrained water ambience on patio/terrace;
- optional score with a slow harmonic build.
Audio should never be required to understand the story or use the site.

## Compliance / content
Do not use Apple logos, copied Apple footage, trademarked product assets or proprietary source code. The visual language can be inspired by premium scrollytelling conventions while the architecture, assets and implementation remain original.
